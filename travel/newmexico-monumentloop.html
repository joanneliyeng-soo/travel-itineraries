DAY 1 · FRIDAY

Caldera to cliff dwellings

Open route in Google Maps ↗
  1. 7:00a

    Leave hotel

    Pack snacks and water; Jemez Springs is the last easy stop for gas.

  2. Drive ~2 hr · I-25 → US-550 → NM-4 Jemez Mountain Trail

    Red cliffs at Jemez Pueblo, then Soda Dam roadside pullout.

  3. 9:00a

    Valles CalderaNational Preserve

    Stamp: Volcano Discovery Center, 4 miles down the entrance road in the Cabin District. Vehicle gate 9 AM – 5 PM most of the year (8 AM – 6 PM Memorial Day to Labor Day).

    Drive-up views of the Valle Grande; the eruption show runs every hour on the half hour.

    ⚠ In winter, snow can close or delay the gate. Check park alerts the night before.
  4. Drive ~30 min · NM-4 → NM-501 into Los Alamos
  5. 10:45a

    Los Alamos · Manhattan ProjectNational Historical Park

    Stamp: Visitor Center, 475 20th St (Fri–Mon 10 AM – 3 PM). Includes one-third of the three-part Los Alamos / Oak Ridge / Hanford stamp.
    ⚠ If the NPS desk is closed, stamps are kept at the Los Alamos County Visitor Center in the same building. Call (505) 661-6277 the week before.

    Lunch in Los Alamos, 11:30 – 12:20.

  6. Drive ~30 min · NM-501 → NM-4
  7. 1:00p

    BandelierNational Monument

    Stamp: Frijoles Canyon Visitor Center, open daily 9 AM – 5 PM (closed Christmas).

    Main Loop Trail's first stretch passes the Big Kiva and Tyuonyi village; the cliff dwellings need ladders, so enjoy them from below.

    ⚠ Mid-May to mid-October, cars arriving 9 AM – 3 PM must park at the White Rock Visitor Center and ride the shuttle in. Off-season, drive straight to the canyon.
  8. 3:00p
    Drive ~1 hr · NM-4 → NM-502 → US-84/285
  9. 4:00p

    Check in · Best Western Plus Inn of Santa Fe

    Evening: dinner around the Santa Fe Plaza, ~15 min north.

DAY 3 · SUNDAY

Inscriptions, lava, petroglyphs

Open route in Google Maps ↗
  1. 7:00a

    Check out, leave with luggage

    Gas up in Grants on the way; stations are sparse west of it.

  2. Drive ~2 hr · I-40 west → exit 81 → NM-53
  3. 9:00a

    El MorroNational Monument

    Stamp: Visitor center, 9 AM – 5 PM. Open daily late May to mid-October; Thursday–Monday the rest of the year (Sunday is always open).

    The half-mile Inscription Loop passes the pool and centuries of carved signatures. Start it by 2:30 PM; trail status (505) 783-4226 x801.

  4. 10:15a
    Drive ~45 min · NM-53 back to I-40 exit 85
  5. 11:00a

    El MalpaisNational Monument

    Stamp: Visitor center, 1900 E Santa Fe Ave, Grants (I-40 exit 85), 9 AM – 5 PM.

    Lunch in Grants, 11:30 – 12:15.

  6. 12:15p
    Drive ~1 hr 15 min · I-40 east → Unser Blvd
  7. 1:30p

    PetroglyphNational Monument

    Stamp: Information Center, 6510 Western Trail NW, 8:30 AM – 4:30 PM. No petroglyphs here; it's the stamp and map stop.
  8. Drive ~5 min · Unser Blvd north
  9. 2:10p

    Boca Negra Canyon

    About 100 petroglyphs close to the parking areas. The Macaw Trail is a 5-minute walk; paths are partly paved but not wheelchair accessible. Last entry 4 PM.

  10. 3:00p
    Drive ~20 min · to Old Town
  11. 3:20p

    Old Town Albuquerque & early dinner

    San Felipe de Neri church and the plaza.

  12. 5:30p

    Return car · Sunport Rental Car Center

    Shuttle to the terminal; aim to be at security by 6:15.

  13. 7:50p

    Depart ABQ → OAK

    Home around 11:30 PM PT.

Before picking dates

  • Skip Thanksgiving and Christmas weekends. Most of these stops close on those holidays.
  • Season changes the plan. Mid-May to mid-October means the Bandelier shuttle; November to April means possible snow on NM-4 and the Valles Caldera gate.
  • Book 2 hotel reservations: the Airport InnSuites for nights 1 and 3, and Santa Fe for night 2.
  • Check hours in the NPS app the week before, especially Valles Caldera and the Manhattan Project desk.
  • Day 3 is an out-and-back on I-40 (El Morro is a dead end off NM-53). It's the only backtrack of the trip.
  • Timezone: New Mexico is one hour ahead of California.
