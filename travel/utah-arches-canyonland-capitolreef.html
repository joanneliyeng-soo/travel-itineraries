If the long weekend includes a holiday

Arches and Island in the Sky list Christmas as their only closed day, so they can take a holiday Day 1. Capitol Reef's visitor center may close on major holidays, so keep it on a non-holiday day, as this plan does.

Arches Visitor Center New park

Stamp: Arches National ParkWinter hours 9:00–4:30 daily, longer the rest of the year; closed only on Christmas.

Timed-entry reservations weren't required in 2026. Check the current rules once dates are set; they have applied in busy months in past years.

12:15

Arches Scenic Drive

Park Avenue viewpoint, Balanced Rock right beside the road, then the Windows section, where the arches are visible from the parking area. Finish at the Delicate Arch lower viewpoint, a short walk from its lot.

Roads and viewpoints can ice over or close after snow. Check the park's alerts that morning.

Island in the Sky Visitor Center New park

Stamp: Canyonlands National ParkWinter hours 9:00–4:00, longer the rest of the year; closed only on Christmas. The Needles district closes in winter, so Island in the Sky is the reliable stop.

The Shafer Canyon overlook is next to the visitor center. Drive on to Grand View Point at the end of the road, where the canyons open up below a short walk from the car.

To do before the trip

  • Once dates are set: book OAK ⇄ SLC flights and the three hotels. Confirm whether Capitol Reef Resort still carries the Best Western name.
  • Once dates are set: reserve a round-trip AWD rental from SLC on Costco Travel.
  • Once dates are set: check whether Arches requires timed entry on your dates.
  • Week before: call Capitol Reef (435-425-3791) for hours, recheck Arches and Canyonlands in the NPS app, and pull NWS forecasts for Moab and Torrey.
  • Each driving morning (winter): check UDOT road conditions. Soldier Summit (US-6) and the high road between Loa and I-70 are the snow risks.
  • Confirm drive times in Google Maps. The ones here come from NPS and travel guides.
