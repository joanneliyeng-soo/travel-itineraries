Heads up

Saturday is the long driving day, about 10 hours at the wheel including the 4½-hour drive back to Vegas from Manzanar. Most of that drive is after dark and crosses Towne Pass, so check road conditions before you leave Lone Pine. Sunday is an easier round trip, about 6 hours.

Hole-in-the-Wall Information Center 2 new stamps

Stamps: Mojave National Preserve + Castle Mountains NMBoth are here. Castle Mountains has no visitor center of its own. A stamp kiosk on the front porch is open 24/7, so these work even if the building is closed.

Picnic lunch with a view of the pocked volcanic walls of Banshee Canyon. The Rings Loop climbs with metal rings, so skip it.

Come in and leave on the paved Essex Road side. NPS warns not to follow GPS onto the dirt roads north of here, even though they look shorter to Vegas.
