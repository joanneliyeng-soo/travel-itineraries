Death Valley Long Weekend

A ready-to-date plan for any three-day weekend: fly into Las Vegas the evening before, cross the Mojave into Death Valley, climb out to Lone Pine and Manzanar, and fly home from Bishop. One direction the whole way.

Two things decide whether this exact plan works

One-way car drop at Bishop. Flying home from Bishop is what lets the route run in one direction. If Costco Travel won't do LAS → BIH or the drop fee is steep, use the fallback on Day 3 and fly home from Las Vegas.

The Bishop flight day and time. Service is daily only in summer and winter. Day 3 is kept loose so any departure after late morning works.

To do before the trip

  • Once dates are set: book The Ranch at Death Valley (or Stovepipe Wells) for Night 1 and the two Best Westerns.
  • Once dates are set: price a one-way LAS pickup / BIH drop on Costco Travel, then call the Bishop counter to confirm a car will be there.
  • Flights: confirm United flies BIH → SFO on your Day 3, then book it with the OAK → LAS flight for Day 0 evening.
  • Week before: recheck hours in the NPS app (Hole-in-the-Wall, Furnace Creek, Manzanar) and the Mojave road conditions page. Pull NWS forecasts for Furnace Creek and Lone Pine; Towne Pass can get snow in winter, and summer heat makes this route unsafe.
  • Confirm drive times in Google Maps. The ones here come from NPS and road guides and are close, not exact.
  • Download offline maps: cell service is spotty or absent in both parks.
