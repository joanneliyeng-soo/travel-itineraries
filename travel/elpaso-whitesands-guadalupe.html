Why the days fall this way

  • Chamizal's visitor center is open Tuesday–Saturday only, so Saturday is its only day this weekend.
  • Guadalupe's Pine Springs visitor center is closed on MLK Day, so it has to be Saturday or Sunday. The route passes it both days, so if wind or snow closes it Saturday, you get a second try Sunday morning.
  • White Sands and Carlsbad Caverns are open all weekend, including MLK Day.
