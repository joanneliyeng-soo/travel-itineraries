Alaska
Denali🚗Private vehicles limited past Mile 15; shuttle/bus deeper in.
Gates of the Arctic✈️Air taxi from Fairbanks, Bettles, or Coldfoot. Zero roads or trails.
Glacier Bay✈️⛵Fly into Gustavus, or arrive by cruise ship/tour boat.
Katmai✈️Float plane from King Salmon, Anchorage, or Homer. Home of the fat bears.
Kenai Fjords🚗⛵Drive to Exit Glacier; the fjords themselves need a boat or air tour.
Kobuk Valley✈️Bush plane from Kotzebue. Least-visited park in the whole system.
Lake Clark✈️⛵Fly or boat from Anchorage, Homer, or Kenai. No road in.
Wrangell–St. Elias🚗✈️Unpaved roads reach McCarthy/Kennicott; interior is fly-in only.
Arizona
Grand Canyon🚗South Rim easy and paved; North Rim seasonal.
Petrified Forest🚗Drive-through park, right off I-40.
Saguaro🚗Two separate districts flanking Tucson.
Arkansas
Hot Springs🚗Sits right in the town of Hot Springs — most urban NP there is.
California
Channel Islands⛵✈️Concessioner ferry or small plane only — no bridge, no roads.
Death Valley🚗Hottest, driest, lowest — but fully drivable.
Joshua Tree🚗Easy drive-in from Palm Springs area.
Kings Canyon🚗Paired with Sequoia; connected by road.
Lassen Volcanic🚗Drive-in, scenic park road.
Pinnacles🚗East and west entrances are NOT connected by road inside the park.
Redwood🚗Strung along Hwy 101, mixed with state park units.
Sequoia🚗Steep, winding mountain road in.
Yosemite🚗Multiple entrances, some seasonal.
Colorado
Black Canyon of the Gunnison🚗South Rim easy; North Rim road rougher, seasonal.
Great Sand Dunes🚗Tallest dunes in North America, right off the parking lot.
Mesa Verde🚗Cliff dwellings mostly require ranger-led tours/ladders once there.
Rocky Mountain🚗Trail Ridge Road is the highest continuous paved road in the US.
Florida
Biscayne🚗⛵Drive to the visitor center — but ~95% of the park is water.
Dry Tortugas⛵✈️Yankee Freedom ferry or seaplane from Key West. No road, ever.
Everglades🚗Multiple separate entrances (Homestead, Shark Valley, Gulf Coast).
Hawaiʻi
Haleakalā🚗Summit road and separate coastal Kīpahulu section.
Hawaiʻi Volcanoes🚗Crater Rim Drive loops the caldera.
Indiana
Indiana Dunes🚗Easy Lake Michigan shoreline access.
Kentucky
Mammoth Cave🚗Surface is drive-in; the cave itself needs a ranger-led tour.
Maine
Acadia🚗Park Loop Road, easy drive-in from Bar Harbor.
Michigan
Isle Royale⛵✈️No cars allowed on the island at all — ferry or seaplane only.
Minnesota
Voyageurs🚗⛵Drive to visitor centers; most of the interior is boat/canoe only.
Missouri
Gateway Arch🚗Smallest NP — it's downtown St. Louis, walkable from anywhere nearby.
Montana
Glacier🚗Going-to-the-Sun Road; vehicle reservation required in peak season.
Nevada
Great Basin🚗Remote but fully paved access; bristlecone pines and Lehman Caves.
New Mexico
Carlsbad Caverns🚗Surface drive-in; cavern via elevator or a walk-in natural entrance trail.
White Sands🚗Gypsum dune drive loop, easy access.
North Carolina / Tennessee
Great Smoky Mountains🚗Most-visited NP in the country; entrances on both NC and TN sides.
North Dakota
Theodore Roosevelt🚗Three separate units, all drive-in.
Ohio
Cuyahoga Valley🚗No entrance fee, easy drive-in between Cleveland and Akron.
Oregon
Crater Lake🚗Rim Drive loops the whole caldera.
South Carolina
Congaree🚗Boardwalk trail right from the visitor center parking lot.
South Dakota
Badlands🚗Loop Road drive-through, dramatic overlooks right off pavement.
Wind Cave🚗Surface drive-in; cave via ranger-led tour.
Texas
Big Bend🚗Remote but paved roads throughout.
Guadalupe Mountains🚗Drive-in; most interior features require hiking to reach.
Utah
Arches🚗Timed-entry reservation required in peak season.
Bryce Canyon🚗Rim drive with numerous overlooks.
Canyonlands🚗Island in the Sky is paved; Needles/Maze districts want high-clearance/4WD.
Capitol Reef🚗Scenic Drive is paved; side roads get rougher.
Zion🚗Mandatory shuttle in main canyon most of the year — your placard is confirmed exempt.
Virginia
Shenandoah🚗Skyline Drive runs the length of the park.
Washington
Mount Rainier🚗Several entrances; Paradise area most accessible.
North Cascades🚗Hwy 20 corridor is drivable; most of the park's interior has no roads.
Olympic🚗Separate entrances reach coast, rainforest, and Hurricane Ridge — not connected internally.
West Virginia
New River Gorge🚗Newest NP (2020); drive-in with famous bridge overlook.
Wyoming (+ MT / ID)
Grand Teton🚗Easy drive-in, right next to Jackson Hole.
Yellowstone🚗Spans WY/MT/ID; multiple entrances, all paved.
American Samoa
National Park of American Samoa✈️⛵Fly to Pago Pago via Honolulu; Ofu and Ta'ū units need an extra inter-island flight or boat.
U.S. Virgin Islands
Virgin Islands✈️⛵Fly to St. Thomas, then passenger ferry to St. John.
