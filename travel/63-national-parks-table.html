🏞️ All 63 National Parks

How to get to each one of them
11/ 63 visited
Alaska
Denali🚗Fairbanks (FAI)Private vehicles limited past Mile 15; shuttle/bus deeper in.
Gates of the Arctic✈️Fairbanks (FAI)Air taxi from Fairbanks, Bettles, or Coldfoot. Zero roads or trails.
Glacier Bay✈️⛵Juneau (JNU)Fly into Gustavus, or arrive by cruise ship/tour boat.
Katmai✈️Anchorage (ANC)Float plane from King Salmon, Anchorage, or Homer. Home of the fat bears.
Kenai Fjords🚗⛵Anchorage (ANC)Drive to Exit Glacier; the fjords themselves need a boat or air tour.
Kobuk Valley✈️Anchorage (ANC)Bush plane from Kotzebue. Least-visited park in the whole system.
Lake Clark✈️⛵Anchorage (ANC)Fly or boat from Anchorage, Homer, or Kenai. No road in.
Wrangell–St. Elias🚗✈️Anchorage (ANC)Unpaved roads reach McCarthy/Kennicott; interior is fly-in only.
Arizona
Grand Canyon🚗Phoenix (PHX)South Rim easy and paved; North Rim seasonal.
Petrified Forest🚗Flagstaff (FLG)Drive-through park, right off I-40.
Saguaro🚗Tucson (TUS)Two separate districts flanking Tucson.
Arkansas
Hot Springs🚗Little Rock (LIT)Sits right in the town of Hot Springs — most urban NP there is.
California
Channel Islands⛵✈️Santa Barbara (SBA)Concessioner ferry or small plane only — no bridge, no roads.
Death Valley🚗Las Vegas (LAS)Hottest, driest, lowest — but fully drivable.
Joshua Tree🚗Palm Springs (PSP)Easy drive-in from Palm Springs area.
Kings Canyon🚗Fresno (FAT)Paired with Sequoia; connected by road.
Lassen Volcanic🚗Redding (RDD)Drive-in, scenic park road.
Pinnacles🚗San Jose (SJC)East and west entrances are NOT connected by road inside the park.
Redwood🚗Arcata/Eureka (ACV)Strung along Hwy 101, mixed with state park units.
Sequoia🚗Fresno (FAT)Steep, winding mountain road in.
Yosemite🚗Fresno (FAT)Multiple entrances, some seasonal.
Colorado
Black Canyon of the Gunnison🚗Montrose (MTJ)South Rim easy; North Rim road rougher, seasonal.
Great Sand Dunes🚗Alamosa (ALS)Tallest dunes in North America, right off the parking lot.
Mesa Verde🚗Durango (DRO)Cliff dwellings mostly require ranger-led tours/ladders once there.
Rocky Mountain🚗Denver (DEN)Trail Ridge Road is the highest continuous paved road in the US.
Florida
Biscayne🚗⛵Miami (MIA)Drive to the visitor center — but ~95% of the park is water.
Dry Tortugas⛵✈️Key West (EYW)Yankee Freedom ferry or seaplane from Key West. No road, ever.
Everglades🚗Miami (MIA)Multiple separate entrances (Homestead, Shark Valley, Gulf Coast).
Hawaiʻi
Haleakalā🚗Kahului (OGG)Summit road and separate coastal Kīpahulu section.
Hawaiʻi Volcanoes🚗Hilo (ITO)Crater Rim Drive loops the caldera.
Indiana
Indiana Dunes🚗Chicago (ORD)Easy Lake Michigan shoreline access.
Kentucky
Mammoth Cave🚗Nashville (BNA)Surface is drive-in; the cave itself needs a ranger-led tour.
Maine
Acadia🚗Bangor (BGR)Park Loop Road, easy drive-in from Bar Harbor.
Michigan
Isle Royale⛵✈️Houghton (CMX)No cars allowed on the island at all — ferry or seaplane only.
Minnesota
Voyageurs🚗⛵International Falls (INL)Drive to visitor centers; most of the interior is boat/canoe only.
Missouri
Gateway Arch🚗St. Louis (STL)Smallest NP — it's downtown St. Louis, walkable from anywhere nearby.
Montana
Glacier🚗Kalispell (FCA)Going-to-the-Sun Road; vehicle reservation required in peak season.
Nevada
Great Basin🚗Las Vegas (LAS)Remote but fully paved access; bristlecone pines and Lehman Caves.
New Mexico
Carlsbad Caverns🚗El Paso (ELP)Surface drive-in; cavern via elevator or a walk-in natural entrance trail.
White Sands🚗El Paso (ELP)Gypsum dune drive loop, easy access.
North Carolina / Tennessee
Great Smoky Mountains🚗Knoxville (TYS)Most-visited NP in the country; entrances on both NC and TN sides.
North Dakota
Theodore Roosevelt🚗Bismarck (BIS)Three separate units, all drive-in.
Ohio
Cuyahoga Valley🚗Cleveland (CLE)No entrance fee, easy drive-in between Cleveland and Akron.
Oregon
Crater Lake🚗Medford (MFR)Rim Drive loops the whole caldera.
South Carolina
Congaree🚗Columbia (CAE)Boardwalk trail right from the visitor center parking lot.
South Dakota
Badlands🚗Rapid City (RAP)Loop Road drive-through, dramatic overlooks right off pavement.
Wind Cave🚗Rapid City (RAP)Surface drive-in; cave via ranger-led tour.
Texas
Big Bend🚗Midland (MAF)Remote but paved roads throughout.
Guadalupe Mountains🚗El Paso (ELP)Drive-in; most interior features require hiking to reach.
Utah
Arches🚗Grand Junction (GJT)Timed-entry reservation required in peak season.
Bryce Canyon🚗Las Vegas (LAS)Rim drive with numerous overlooks.
Canyonlands🚗Moab (CNY)Island in the Sky is paved; Needles/Maze districts want high-clearance/4WD.
Capitol Reef🚗Grand Junction (GJT)Scenic Drive is paved; side roads get rougher.
Zion🚗Las Vegas (LAS)Mandatory shuttle in main canyon most of the year — your placard is confirmed exempt.
Virginia
Shenandoah🚗Washington Dulles (IAD)Skyline Drive runs the length of the park.
Washington
Mount Rainier🚗Seattle (SEA)Several entrances; Paradise area most accessible.
North Cascades🚗Seattle (SEA)Hwy 20 corridor is drivable; most of the park's interior has no roads.
Olympic🚗Seattle (SEA)Separate entrances reach coast, rainforest, and Hurricane Ridge — not connected internally.
West Virginia
New River Gorge🚗Charleston, WV (CRW)Newest NP (2020); drive-in with famous bridge overlook.
Wyoming (+ MT / ID)
Grand Teton🚗Jackson Hole (JAC)Easy drive-in, right next to Jackson Hole.
Yellowstone🚗Bozeman (BZN)Spans WY/MT/ID; multiple entrances, all paved.
American Samoa
National Park of American Samoa✈️⛵Pago Pago (PPG)Fly to Pago Pago via Honolulu; Ofu and Ta'ū units need an extra inter-island flight or boat.
U.S. Virgin Islands
Virgin Islands✈️⛵St. Thomas (STT)Fly to St. Thomas, then passenger ferry to St. John.
