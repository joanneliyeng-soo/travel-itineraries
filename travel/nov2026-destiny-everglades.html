Before you go

  • Move Car 2's drop-off from Tue Dec 1 to Mon Nov 30 at MIA, or cancel it once all four stamps are in hand on Tuesday (free cancellation).
  • Check passport expiry for all four.
  • Confirm the cruise port arrival time on the DCL app once it opens, and the Bahamas port order.
  • Late November is peak dry season — highs in the low 80s, few mosquitoes.
  • Every stop is a building you park next to. No trails, no boardwalks, no tram.
