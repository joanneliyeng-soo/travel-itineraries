Across the Rockies

Best on Memorial Day or Labor Day weekend · mountain passes and short visitor-center hours make it risky the rest of the year

Fly into Denver, drive west across Colorado and into Utah, and fly home from Salt Lake City. Eight new stamps in three days, five of them national parks. Stamp stops run back-to-back each morning; sightseeing only happens at each day's last park, and the long drives wait until the parks close.

Why the days fall this way

  • Florissant's visitor center is closed Mondays and Tuesdays, so it has to be Day 1 on a Sat–Mon weekend. If you add a PTO day, add it to the end.
  • Curecanti is the tightest stamp. You arrive around 3:15, and Elk Creek has kept weekday-only, 4 PM-close hours outside summer. Confirm its summer weekend hours before booking.
  • Capitol Reef falls on the holiday Monday. Its visitor center has closed on some federal holidays in past years, so confirm Monday hours in the NPS app.
  • Each morning starts at a visitor center's opening time, so one late start pushes the whole day.

To do once dates are set

  • Pick the weekend: Memorial Day or Labor Day fits best (Sat–Mon, summer hours, open passes).
  • Book: OAK → DEN and SLC → OAK flights, a one-way DEN → SLC rental on Costco Travel, and hotels near DEN, in Montrose and in Moab.
  • Confirm by phone: Curecanti's Elk Creek Saturday hours, and Capitol Reef's holiday-Monday hours.
  • Check Arches timed entry for Day 2.
  • Week before: recheck all eight visitor centers in the NPS app and pull NWS forecasts for Poncha Pass and Blue Mesa.
