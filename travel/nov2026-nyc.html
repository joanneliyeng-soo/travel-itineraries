Before You Go

  • Book the 9:00 AM ferry from Battery Park for Sun Nov 8 on statuecitycruises.com. Buy only from the official site or the windows inside Castle Clinton.
  • Call Stonewall at (212) 355-6295 to confirm Sunday hours.
  • Check Federal Hall's hours in the NPS app.
  • Ask BW Soho to hold your bags until about 2:30 PM Sunday.
  • Ask Hotel Henri to hold your bags Saturday morning after checkout.
  • Check every Uber leg in Google Maps the night before. Times on this page are planning estimates.
  • Confirm your JFK terminal for AS 41 in the Alaska app.
  • Pack your passport book and a pen. Ask at every stop for any bonus cancellations.
