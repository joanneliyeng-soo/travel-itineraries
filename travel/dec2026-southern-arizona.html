Before you go

Holiday hours and checks

New Year's Day closures

Organ Pipe and Tumacácori stay open Jan 1. Coronado, Chiricahua and Fort Bowie visitor centers closed Jan 1 last year, so all three are on Jan 2.

Time zone

Arizona stays on MST all year, one hour ahead of California in winter.

Hotels

Book around Dec 17 along with the rental car: Goodyear (Dec 31), Sierra Vista (Jan 1), near PHX (Jan 2).

Week-before checks

Confirm all five visitor center hours in the NPS app, especially Fort Bowie and Organ Pipe.
