Flights

  • Out (Day 0): Breeze runs a summer-seasonal SFO → GJT nonstop on limited days (it launched on Wed/Sat). If it doesn't fall on a Friday, United SFO → DEN → GJT.
  • Home (Day 3): DRO → DEN → SFO on United, or DRO → PHX → SFO/OAK on American. Aim for a mid-afternoon departure or later.

Car & hotels

  • Rental: one-way GJT pickup, DRO drop via Costco Travel. Check the one-way drop fee.
  • Fri: Best Western Grande River Inn & Suites, Clifton (Grand Junction)
  • Sat: SureStay Plus by Best Western Montrose
  • Sun: Best Western Turquoise Inn & Suites, 535 E Main St, Cortez

When to go

  • Best: Memorial Day (May 29–31, 2027) through Labor Day (Sep 4–6, 2027). All visitor centers run daily hours and Black Canyon's South Rim Drive is open.
  • Fall color: Columbus Day (Oct 9–11, 2027) works, but Curecanti's Elk Creek VC has cut to weekdays-only in past Octobers and the Mesa Verde Museum shortens hours from Oct 22. Re-check before choosing it.

Pass & access

  • America the Beautiful + Access Pass cover entrance fees at Colorado NM, Black Canyon and Mesa Verde.
  • Every stop is a visitor center, scenic drive or roadside overlook. No cliff-dwelling tours (ladders) and no trails needed.

Hovenweep Visitor Center

Hovenweep National Monument

Stamp inside the VC by the Square Tower Group. A short walk from the VC reaches the first canyon overlook toward Hovenweep Castle.

VC hours have been cut for staffing before (one season ran 10–3, closed Tue/Wed). A Sunday early-afternoon arrival is the safe window. Confirm in the NPS app or at (970) 233-9167. The VC is in Utah but on Mountain Time like the rest of the trip.

Before booking

  • Price the one-way GJT → DRO rental on Costco Travel; the drop fee decides whether open-jaw still makes sense.
  • Check whether Breeze's seasonal SFO–GJT days line up with a Friday departure.
  • Confirm every visitor center's hours in the NPS app the week before, especially Hovenweep and Elk Creek.
  • Check Black Canyon alerts for any remaining South Rim Fire closures.
  • Verify each drive leg in Google Maps. Times here come from published sources and are rounded.
  • Altitude runs 8,000–10,200 ft on Days 1–2; pack layers and water (Black Canyon has no water October–April).
  • Optional add-on if the flight is late: Aztec Ruins NM is south of Durango, but it may belong with the Albuquerque placeholder instead.
