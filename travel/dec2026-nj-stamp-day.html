🚗 ~20 min · Directions
10:00 AM

Thomas Edison NHP NHP

211 Main St, West Orange. Arrive right at opening.

◈ Stamp station Laboratory Complex Visitor Center (Building 1). Every visitor starts here. Open Thu–Sun, 10 AM–4 PM.

~45 min. The Main Lab has an elevator between floors. Glenmont (the Edison home) is closed until further notice.

🚗 ~20–25 min · Directions
11:10 AM

Paterson Great Falls NHP NHP

72 McBride Ave Extension, Paterson. Park in the Great Falls Overlook lot right beside it.

◈ Stamp station Administrative office at the main address. There's no visitor center. Rangers are on site 9 AM–4:30 PM daily.

⚠ Saturday office hours aren't confirmed online. Call 973-523-0370 the week before.

~30 min. See the 77-ft falls from Overlook Park. Portable toilets only; there are no indoor restrooms.

11:45 AM

Lunch, then head west on I-80

Eat in the Paterson area or at a stop along I-80 before the quieter stretch toward the Gap.

🚗 ~1 hr 05 min via I-80 W · Directions
1:20 PM

Delaware Water Gap NRA NRA

Kittatinny Point Visitor Center, off I-80 at Exit 1, Columbia, NJ. This is the farthest point of the day.

◈ Stamp station Kittatinny Point Visitor Center. The stamp reads "Delaware Water Gap NRA · Kittatinny Point, NJ." Open Saturdays 9 AM–4 PM; closed Mon–Fri (per the NPS app).

~30 min. Views of the Gap from the visitor center. The Bushkill (PA) HQ desk is weekdays only in winter, so this is the Saturday option.

🚗 ~1 hr · Directions
2:50 PM

Morristown NHP NHP

Jockey Hollow, 586 Tempe Wick Rd. This is where the Continental Army wintered in 1779–80.

◈ Stamp station Jockey Hollow Visitor Center. Open Thu–Sun, 10 AM–4 PM. The winter closure doesn't start until Jan 4, 2027.

⚠ Closes at 4 PM. If you're running late, shorten lunch: this is the stop with the least buffer (~70 min).

~30 min. Washington's Headquarters Museum in town is a second stamp location if Jockey Hollow doesn't work.

Reminders
  • Pack your Mid-Atlantic passport pages. All four stamps go in the New Jersey and Pennsylvania sections.
  • Book the rental and the EWR hotel. Use Costco Travel for the car.
  • Call Paterson the week before (973-523-0370) to confirm the office is open Saturday.
  • Check the NWS forecast around Dec 9. Mid-December averages are ~46°F / 32°F, with a small chance of snow. If snow or ice is forecast, drop Kittatinny Point (the long I-80 stretch) and keep the three close stops.
  • Re-check hours in the NPS app the week before, especially Kittatinny Point and Jockey Hollow.
  • Start late enough to avoid morning ice. Overnight lows are near freezing, so a ~9:15 car pickup works.
