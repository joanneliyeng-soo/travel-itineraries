Reminders

  • Pick dates: any Thursday–Sunday, plus Wednesday-night flight. Two PTO days. Avoid weeks with a federal holiday on Thu/Fri.
  • Flights: SFO → IND (United nonstop) Wednesday evening; CLT → SFO Sunday ~5 PM or later.
  • Car: one-way rental IND → CLT via Costco Travel. Watch the drop fee.
  • Hotels (3 nights in route): Murfreesboro, Somerset, Gatlinburg. Best Western first choice. Plus one night by IND airport.
  • Time zones: Day 1 flips ET → CT → ET → CT. Russell Cave (AL) is Central; Chattanooga onward is Eastern.
  • Hard cutoffs: Lincoln Boyhood before noon (Day 1), Oak Ridge by 4 (Day 2), Mill Springs and Cumberland Gap by 4 (Day 3), Andrew Johnson by 4 (Day 3).
  • Re-check hours in the NPS app the week before, especially Bandy Creek, Russell Cave, Sugarlands and Carl Sandburg (post-Helene hours have shifted).
  • Drive times are from Google Maps (checked Nov 2026). Day totals don't include ~20 min per stamp stop.
  • Optional bonus: Big South Fork's Stearns Depot stamp (KY) is open only Thu–Sun in summer. Not needed for the unit.
