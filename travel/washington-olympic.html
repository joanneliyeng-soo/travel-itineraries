Washington Long Weekend

A ready-to-date loop for a late-spring to early-fall three-day weekend: Paradise on Mount Rainier on Day 1, across the water to Olympic on Day 2, and the Klondike's gold in Seattle on Day 3. Two national parks, three new stamps, no backtracking.

The one scheduling rule

Klondike Gold Rush is open Wednesday–Sunday, so Day 3 needs to land on one of those days. Day 0 on a Thursday is the cleanest fit, putting Day 3 on a Sunday.

Mount Rainier has used timed-entry reservations for the Paradise area in some recent summers. Check once dates are set.

To do before the trip

  • Once dates are set: confirm Day 3 falls Wednesday–Sunday, then book the National Park Inn for Night 1 first. Summer rooms go months ahead.
  • Once dates are set: check whether Mount Rainier needs a timed-entry reservation on your dates.
  • Once dates are set: book Olympic Lodge by Ayres and the Best Western at SeaTac.
  • Rental: reserve a round-trip car through Costco Travel.
  • Morning of each park day: glance at road status. Rainier 360-569-2211, Hurricane Ridge 360-565-3131. Summer wildfire smoke can also close roads or hide the views.
  • Week before: recheck visitor center hours in the NPS app and pull NWS forecasts for Paradise and Port Angeles.
  • Confirm drive times in Google Maps. The ones here come from NPS and travel guides.
