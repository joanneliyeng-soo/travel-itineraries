Day 1 is the tight one

Three NPS visitor centers across about 370 miles. Fossil Butte closes at 5:30 pm in summer, so the latest safe arrival is about 4:45.

If the flight lands after about 10:30, drop Timpanogos (it's 40 minutes the wrong way) and keep Golden Spike and Fossil Butte.
